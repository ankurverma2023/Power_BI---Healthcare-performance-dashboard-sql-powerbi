## ✨ Overview
A simulated engagement for a 127-hospital group across 10 countries. I modelled the data in SQL Server (star schema, 50+ analytical queries) and built a 6-page Power BI dashboard with 70+ DAX measures.

| 230K | 750K | 203K | 10 | 6 |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Admissions | Billing lines | Claims | Countries | Report pages |

## 🎯 Key Outcomes
| Insight | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 💸 Avoidable claim rejections | **$21.96M** (60.5% of rejections were fixable process gaps) |
| ⏳ Aged receivables | **92.7%** of unpaid balances are over 90 days old |
| 🧓 Readmissions | Patients 60+ drive **65.1%** of readmissions |
| 📈 Revenue profile | Top 5% of bills = **61%** of revenue |
| ✅ Data trust | Dashboard KPIs validated against SQL queries |

## 📊 Report Pages
Executive Overview · Patient & Admissions · Billing & Revenue · Insurance & Claims · Hospital & Doctor Performance · Disease, Medicine & Lab Insights

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
**SQL Server:** CTEs, window functions, PIVOT, views, stored procedures
**Power BI:** star-schema modelling, DAX, time intelligence, calculated columns, theming
